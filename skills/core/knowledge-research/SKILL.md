# Knowledge Research

Use this Skill when the answer should be grounded in the business's stored Knowledge.

## Workflow

1. Turn the request into two or three focused search queries, including important synonyms.
2. Query Knowledge and inspect the most relevant Pages rather than relying only on result snippets.
3. Distinguish facts stated by the Pages from conclusions you infer across them.
4. When Pages disagree, say what conflicts and prefer the more specific or recently maintained
   source when the available metadata supports that choice.
5. Answer the user's question directly, then cite every Page that materially supports the answer.

## Quality Bar

- Do not claim Knowledge contains a fact that appears only in the user's prompt or your general
  knowledge.
- Do not pad the answer with weakly related Pages.
- If the search finds no adequate source, say so plainly and suggest the smallest useful next step,
  such as adding or updating a Page.
