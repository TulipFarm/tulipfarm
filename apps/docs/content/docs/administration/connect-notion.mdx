---
title: Connect Notion
description: Create a Notion integration, paste its token, and share the pages agents may use.
---

This guide connects the bundled `notion` integration. It lets agents search your workspace,
read and write pages, and query databases.

Notion access is **per-page**. The token grants nothing on its own — you choose what it can
reach by sharing pages with it. Skipping that step leaves you with a valid connection where
every search comes back empty.

## Outcome

At the end, Notion shows as connected, eight Notion tools are available to your agents, and
the pages you shared are readable and writable from chat.

## Create the Notion integration

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open **Integrations → notion** and follow the connect flow. It links out to
    `https://www.notion.so/my-integrations`.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose **New integration**, name it `TulipFarm`, and pick the workspace it should serve.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set **Content Capabilities** to Read, Update, and Insert content. Leave user information
    off — agents do not need it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect the token

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the integration you just created and go to its **Configuration** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Copy the **Internal Integration Secret**. It starts with `ntn_`, or `secret_` on older
    integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Paste it into the **Internal Integration Secret** field (`NOTION_ACCESS_TOKEN`) in TulipFarm
    and save. It is encrypted in the secrets store.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An internal integration rather than OAuth: Notion's OAuth flow is for public integrations
serving many workspaces and requires submitting yours to Notion for review. A single-workspace
deployment gets the same API and the same permissions without it.

## Share the pages agents may use

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In Notion, open a page you want agents to work with.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the **•••** menu → **Connections** → choose **TulipFarm**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Everything nested under that page is shared too, so one top-level page is usually enough.
    Databases are shared the same way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

Return to **integrations** and confirm Notion shows connected. Then ask in chat:

```prompt
Search Notion for the onboarding page and tell me what's on it.
```

Writes are approval-gated like any other change an agent makes, so this asks you to confirm
before anything reaches Notion:

```prompt
Add a "Follow-ups" heading to the bottom of the onboarding page in Notion.
```

## If Notion tools return nothing

Almost always the sharing step. A token with no shared pages is valid — it just sees an empty
workspace. Open the page in Notion and check that its ••• → **Connections** list includes
TulipFarm.
