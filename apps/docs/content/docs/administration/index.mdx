---
title: Administering TulipFarm
description: Set up a TulipFarm instance for your team. Connect an AI model, manage credentials, invite people, connect Slack and GitHub, and review what agents did.
---

A running server is not yet a usable product. This track covers the settings that turn a fresh
instance into one your team can work in: which AI model it uses, who may sign in, what it is
connected to, and how you check up on it.

Everything here happens in the TulipFarm interface, in the areas that admin accounts can reach.
It does not require a terminal.
